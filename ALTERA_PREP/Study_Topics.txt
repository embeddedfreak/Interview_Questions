Very important interview question: Mutex vs semaphore?

Mutex is primarily for mutual exclusion and has ownership semantics.

Semaphore is a signaling/synchronization primitive and does not have mutex-style ownership.

Another important question: Can you use a mutex inside an interrupt handler?

No. A mutex can sleep, whereas a hard interrupt handler cannot sleep.
